Neural Architecture & System Specifications

Technical blueprint of the multimodal surveillance framework constructed for final-year project defense.

1

Visual Temporal Stream

Video clips (16 frames @ 224×224) pass through a ResNet-50 backbone to extract 2048-dimensional deep feature embeddings. A 2-layer Bidirectional GRU followed by a ResNet Residual Block learns spatio-temporal dynamics and classifies 7 XD-Violence incident categories.

Input: (B, 16, 2048) → Output: 7 Class Logits
2

Acoustic YAMNet Stream

Surveillance audio is converted to 16 kHz mono waveforms. The YAMNet acoustic model extracts 1024-dimensional frame-level embeddings. Mean-Max Pooling captures sustained acoustic context alongside transient explosive bursts like gunshots and impacts.

Input: (T, 1024) → Mean-Max (2048) → 7 Probs
3

Late Decision Fusion

Combines independent predictions with dynamic weighting α. In low-light or occluded scenes, acoustic signals compensate; in noisy scenes, visual features dominate. YOLOv8 simultaneously tags suspicious objects and weapons in real-time.

P_fused = α · P_video + (1 - α) · P_audio
