5-Step Implementation Roadmap
Step 1: Environment & Project Setup: Initialize Python FastAPI workspace (FastAPI, OpenCV, YOLO, Librosa, PyTorch, Gemini API).
Step 2: Live CCTV Engine: Build multi-threaded frame reader (stream_ingest.py), YOLO hazard detector (detector.py), and Gemini VLM frame summarizer.
Step 3: Voice Validation Engine: Build speaker biometrics module (biometrics.py), liveness checker (anti_spoof.py), and Whisper stress analyzer.
Step 4: Cross-Modal Backend: Fast-API REST routes & WebSockets to stream live video overlays, voice scores, and alerts.
Step 5: Interactive Dashboard: Web UI with live CCTV video player grid, mic recorder widget, and incident ticker.
